\subsection{Pre-lab Work}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item In a microwell plate, combine 2 drops each of \ce{BaCl2} and \ce{Al2(SO4)3}.
    \item Write a balanced chemical equation for the reaction.
    \item Design a procedure that will allow you to experimentally determine the chemical formula of the precipitate, without consulting the datasheet or theory.
    \item Carry out your procedure and record observations.
\end{enumerate}
